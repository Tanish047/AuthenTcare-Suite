FDA 510(k) Premarket Notification Requirements

A 510(k) is a premarket submission made to FDA to demonstrate that the device to be marketed is as safe and effective, that is, substantially equivalent (SE) to a legally marketed device (predicate device) that is not subject to PMA.

Key Requirements:
1. Device description and intended use
2. Substantial equivalence comparison to predicate device
3. Performance data demonstrating safety and effectiveness
4. Proposed labeling
5. Risk analysis per ISO 14971

The 510(k) process typically takes 90 days for FDA review, though this can be extended if additional information is requested.

Class II medical devices typically require 510(k) clearance before marketing in the United States.
